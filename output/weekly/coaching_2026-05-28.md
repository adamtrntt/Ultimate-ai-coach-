## 3. Coaching Notes for Next Week

### 1. 🎯 Enforce a Hard HR Cap on Easy Runs: **152 bpm maximum**
Your easy run HRs (144–147 bpm) are trending better this week — keep going. Your Z1/Z2 boundary sits at ~152 bpm. On any session labelled "easy," if your watch reads 153+, **slow down immediately — walk if needed.** At 102kg in Canberra winter mornings (15–20°C), there's no excuse for easy runs drifting into Z2/Z3. These sessions build the aerobic engine for August; running them too hard just means you're tired for your quality sessions.

### 2. 📏 Saturday Long Run: Progressive Means Patient
If the 15km progressive long run is still ahead, start the first 5km genuinely easy — **target 5:50–6:00/km, HR under 145 bpm.** The "progressive" element should only feel like proper marathon pace (4:58/km) in the **final 3–4km**, and only if HR and legs allow. Last week's 13km was run at 161 bpm average — that's a tempo effort disguised as a long run. Resist the urge to push early.

### 3. 💤 Sleep is a training session — treat it like one
Seven consecutive nights of sleep quality rated 2/5, with sleep scores mostly in the 75–85 range and one night at just 16 (May 1). You're building a 13-week block — chronic sleep deficit will blunt adaptation, elevate injury risk, and suppress HRV. **Prioritise 7.5–8 hours per night.** Set a consistent lights-out time and protect it. This is as important as your Wednesday tempo session.

### 4. ⏱️ Cadence: Work Toward 80 spm on Easy Runs
Your easy run cadence is sitting at **77–78 spm** — below the 80 spm threshold that reduces ground contact time and injury risk (especially relevant at 102kg). On next week's easy runs, try a 5-minute block in the middle where you consciously quicken your turnover. You don't need a metronome — just think "quick feet, light contact." Your tempo run already hit **81 spm**, proving you have it in the tank.

### 5. 🌡️ Heat Management: Morning Windows Are Your Best Asset
Canberra in late May/early June means temperatures will drop further — take advantage of it. Sessions logging 20–30°C ambient (likely afternoon/midday) are costing you HR efficiency. Your May 25 easy run at **15–18°C morning** produced your best HR control of the block (145 bpm avg). **Lock in morning sessions** — even 30 minutes earlier can mean 3–5°C cooler and meaningfully lower HR at the same pace.
