## 3. Coaching Notes for Next Week

**1. Protect the easy runs — enforce a HR cap of 148 bpm**
Monday and Thursday easy runs are both drifting to 144–145 bpm average, which is fine but close to the ceiling. If next week's Runna easy sessions are similar distances, cap effort at 148 bpm hard. If HR climbs above that, slow down — including walk breaks if needed. The ATL is at 35+ coming into this week; the easy days are your recovery, not bonus fitness days. Running them too hard compounds fatigue.

**2. Bump cadence to 80 spm as a process target on all runs**
Every run this week sat at 77–78 spm. At sub-3:30 race pace (4:58/km), 80–82 spm is the efficiency sweet spot for a 102 kg runner — shorter ground contact time reduces impact load per stride, which matters over 42km. Pick one drill: count steps for 30 seconds and multiply by 2 at the start of each run, then aim to tick up 1–2 spm. No need to force it — just make it a conscious cue. Check the cadence field after each session.

**3. Prioritise sleep — aim for 7.5+ hours before Thursday and Saturday**
Sleep scores this week ranged 72–85 with quality ratings mostly 2–3 out of 5. With Form negative and ATL elevated, sleep is the primary recovery lever available. If the Saturday long run is again 15–18km (typical week 3 progression), you need two solid nights before it, not one. Target lights-out 30 minutes earlier than usual on Wednesday and Friday nights specifically. HRV has been suppressed post-long run (42 on 23 May post-Saturday) — better sleep will accelerate that recovery bounce.

**4. On the tempo session — monitor max HR and don't exceed 178 bpm**
Last week's tempo hit 176 bpm, which is 98% of LTHR. That's appropriate for over-and-under work, but with accumulated fatigue going into week 3, there's a risk of overshooting if you go in feeling sluggish and try to compensate with pace. Set a ceiling of 178 bpm on any interval peak. If HR climbs above that in the first interval, back off pace by 5–8 seconds/km — the adaptation still occurs, and you protect the Saturday long run.

**5. Don't add anything extra this week**
The compliance record is excellent (4/4). The temptation after a good week is to add a fifth run or extend a session. Resist it. CTL is 23.5, ATL is 35+, Form is roughly −12. The body is absorbing load right now — adding unplanned stress in this window increases injury risk without adding fitness. Trust the Runna plan, execute the four sessions, and let the training sink in.
