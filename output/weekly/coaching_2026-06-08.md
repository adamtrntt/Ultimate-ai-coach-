## 3. Coaching Notes for Next Week

**1. Nail the tempo session — but respect the HR ceiling on the 2km@4:25 efforts**
Your over-and-under session on May 27 showed you can run 4:43–4:59/km at HR 153–161 bpm. The planned 4:25/km reps will push you closer to 165–172 bpm — that's fine for a quality session, but don't chase the pace if HR spikes above 175 bpm in the first rep. The risk at Form -16 is going too deep too early. Execute rep 1 conservatively, assess, then commit on rep 2.

**2. Cadence is your free performance gain — target 82–84 spm on all runs**
Your cadence has been sitting at 77–79 spm across every run in this block. At 102kg, a low cadence combined with Canberra's undulating terrain increases ground contact stress and injury risk. Even a 3–4 spm increase reduces loading on your joints meaningfully. On the Thursday easy run in particular, consciously bump to 82 spm. Use a metronome or Garmin cadence alert if needed — don't wait for race week to address this.

**3. The Saturday long run is the week's key session — execute the structure, not the ego pace**
The plan calls for 5km easy → 5km @ 5:20 → 8km @ 5:00 → 1km easy. Your 15km progressive run (May 30) drifted to avg HR 152 with the athlete rating feel as 1/5. That's a warning. For the 8km@5:00 segment, target HR no higher than **165 bpm** and back off pace to 5:10 if HR is climbing above that by the 4km mark. 5:00/km is your target marathon pace minus 2 seconds — don't trash yourself 10 weeks out.

**4. Sleep is a training variable — treat it as one**
Your two worst sleep nights (Jun 6: 6.4 hrs, score 67 | May 30: 6.8 hrs, score 77) both preceded or coincided with your biggest run days. HRV dropped to 46 on Jun 5 after several poor nights. Before Saturday's long run, prioritise **7.5–8.5 hours on Friday night**. If HRV is below 50 Saturday morning and you slept poorly, drop the 8km@5:00 segment to 5:10/km without guilt — the aerobic volume is still the win.

**5. Easy means easy — HR cap on Monday and Thursday**
Your easy runs are landing at 138–145 bpm average, which is acceptable but trending toward the upper end given your LTHR of 180. In Canberra winter, you have a rare opportunity to run cooler and keep HR lower. On both easy days, set a **HR cap of 145 bpm** and back off pace if you exceed it — even if that means running 5:50–6:00/km. These sessions exist to absorb the week's quality work, not add to it.
